# Syndes learner experience

The student-facing side of Syndes: working through locally stored learning material, one task at a time, including without a connection.

## Content

**Module**:
A self-contained unit of teacher-prepared learning material: a summary, learning outcomes, an ordered set of Lessons, and optionally a Quiz and a Flashcard deck.
_Avoid_: Course, unit, pack

**Lesson**:
One reading step in a Module's sequence, made of titled sections.
_Avoid_: Chapter, page, article

**Quiz**:
The short set of questions at the end of a Module, answered one Question at a time.
_Avoid_: Test, exam, assessment

**Question**:
One item in a Quiz, answered either by choosing an option or by entering text.
_Avoid_: Item, problem

**Result**:
The score and per-Question review a Learner sees after submitting a Quiz, scored on the device.
_Avoid_: Grade, mark

**Flashcard deck**:
A teacher-approved set of Flashcards that belongs to a Module.
_Avoid_: Card set, stack

**Flashcard**:
One study card with a front and a back, where the back holds teacher-approved study text.
_Avoid_: Card (on its own)

## People

**Learner**:
The student using Syndes to study Modules and complete Quizzes.
_Avoid_: User, profile

**Teacher**:
The person who prepares and reviews Module content before Learners use it.
_Avoid_: Author, admin

**Administrator**:
A person authorized to grant or remove Teacher access.
_Avoid_: Teacher

**Account**:
A person's Syndes identity, used to determine their access to learning and teaching activities.
_Avoid_: Profile

## Place and state

**Library**:
The set of Modules stored on this device.
_Avoid_: Catalog, store

**Progress**:
A Learner's saved place and answers for a Module, kept on this device only.
_Avoid_: Profile, history, account data

**Continue**:
Resuming a Module at the Learner's saved place in its Progress.
_Avoid_: Resume, pick up

**Completed**:
The state of a Module after the Learner has reached its final Lesson and, when the Module has a Quiz, submitted it.
_Avoid_: Finished, done, passed

**Ready offline**:
The state of a Module whose complete content is stored on this device.
_Avoid_: Downloaded, cached, synced

## Teacher side

**Draft**:
A Teacher's unsealed Module, saved on the Teacher's device. It holds correct answers in the clear and never reaches a Learner.
_Avoid_: Unpublished module, work in progress

**Seal**:
Turning a Draft into the Module file Learners open, with each correct answer replaced by a check the device can run. The word stays in Teacher screens only.
_Avoid_: Publish, compile, encrypt

**Section**:
A group of Learners taught together, such as Grade 6 Sampaguita.
_Avoid_: Class (for the group itself), batch

**Class**:
One Section taught one learning area by the Teacher, such as Science for Grade 6 Sampaguita.
_Avoid_: Course, subject (for the pairing)

**Score**:
A Learner's raw total in one grading component for a quarter: written work, performance tasks, or the quarterly assessment.
_Avoid_: Mark, points (for the total)

**Grade**:
The quarterly grade a Teacher records for a Learner in a Class, computed from weighted Scores and transmuted under DepEd Order No. 8, s. 2015. A Grade is not a Result; Results come from Module Quizzes.
_Avoid_: Mark, rating

**Schedule**:
The Teacher's recurring weekly periods: classes, advisory, and consultation hours.
_Avoid_: Timetable, calendar
