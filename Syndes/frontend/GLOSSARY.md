# Syndes learner experience

The student-facing side of Syndes: working through locally stored learning material, one task at a time, including without a connection.

## Content

**Module**:
A self-contained unit of teacher-prepared learning material: a summary, learning outcomes, an ordered set of Lessons, and optionally a Quiz and a Flashcard deck.
_Avoid_: Course, unit, pack

**Lesson**:
One reading step in a Module's sequence, made of titled sections.
_Avoid_: Chapter, page, article

**Quiz**:
The short set of questions at the end of a Module, answered one Question at a time.
_Avoid_: Test, exam, assessment

**Question**:
One item in a Quiz, answered either by choosing an option or by entering text.
_Avoid_: Item, problem

**Result**:
The score and per-Question review a Learner sees after submitting a Quiz, scored on the device.
_Avoid_: Grade, mark

**Flashcard deck**:
A teacher-approved set of Flashcards that belongs to a Module.
_Avoid_: Card set, stack

**Flashcard**:
One study card with a front and a back, where the back holds teacher-approved study text.
_Avoid_: Card (on its own)

## People

**Learner**:
The student using Syndes on a device. A Learner has no account.
_Avoid_: User, profile, account

**Teacher**:
The person who prepares and reviews Module content before Learners use it.
_Avoid_: Author, admin

## Place and state

**Library**:
The set of Modules stored on this device.
_Avoid_: Catalog, store

**Progress**:
A Learner's saved place and answers for a Module, kept on this device only.
_Avoid_: Profile, history, account data

**Continue**:
Resuming a Module at the Learner's saved place in its Progress.
_Avoid_: Resume, pick up

**Completed**:
The state of a Module after the Learner has reached its final Lesson and, when the Module has a Quiz, submitted it.
_Avoid_: Finished, done, passed

**Ready offline**:
The state of a Module whose complete content is stored on this device.
_Avoid_: Downloaded, cached, synced
